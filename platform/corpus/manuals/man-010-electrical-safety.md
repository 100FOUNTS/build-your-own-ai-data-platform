# Electrical safety for pump motors

## Voltage and risks
The plant motors run at 480 V three-phase. Contact with live parts can be fatal. All electrical work requires LOTO, qualified personnel and verification of the absence of voltage.

## Personal protective equipment
Dielectric helmet, arc-rated face shield, class 0 dielectric gloves with leather protectors, dielectric footwear and arc-rated clothing. Inspect the gloves before every use.

## Insulation test
Before re-energizing a motor after a repair or after a long shutdown, measure the insulation with a megohmmeter at 500 V. The minimum acceptable value is 100 megohms between each phase and ground. Between 10 and 100 megohms the motor must be dried and tested again; below 10 megohms it must not be energized.

## Abnormal current
A sustained current above 110 % of rated indicates overload, misalignment or an incipient mechanical failure. Above 125 % the protection must trip.

## Distances
Keep at least 1 meter away from any open live panel and always work with a colleague present.
