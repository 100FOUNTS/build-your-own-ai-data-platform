# Bearings of the CP-200 and CP-350 pumps

## Function and signs of wear
The bearings support the rotor shaft and absorb the radial and axial load. A healthy bearing produces an almost constant vibration at the running frequency (1×). When wear appears on the outer or inner race, vibration components show up at fault frequencies and harmonics, and the overall vibration (RMS) rises gradually over weeks.

## Operating limits
Overall vibration is assessed according to the ISO 10816 standard. For these pumps the alarm triggers at 4.5 mm/s RMS and the trip (protective shutdown) at 7.1 mm/s RMS. The maximum temperature in the bearing housing is 85 °C in continuous operation; above 95 °C the pump must be stopped.

## What to do with a rising trend
If vibration grows more than 30 % over the baseline of the last 28 days, schedule an inspection within 48 hours at most. Check the lubrication, the coupling alignment and looseness of the base. If the bearing shows bluish discoloration or a metallic noise, replace the complete bearing set and the oil seal in the same job.

## Service life
The L10 rated life of the bearings is 40 000 operating hours at rated load. Operating away from the best efficiency point reduces that life significantly.
