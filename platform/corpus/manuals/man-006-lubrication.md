# Bearing lubrication

## Grease type
The CP-200 and CP-350 pumps use NLGI grade 2 grease with a lithium base and mineral oil. Do not mix it with greases with another thickener (for example polyurea), because it can soften and lose lubricity.

## Amount and interval
Apply 30 grams per bearing every 2000 operating hours, or every 3 months, whichever comes first. In environments above 70 °C reduce the interval to 1000 hours. Too much grease raises the temperature and is as harmful as too little.

## Procedure
1. Clean the grease fitting before connecting the gun.
2. With the pump running, apply the grease slowly (two or three strokes).
3. Leave the drain plug open for 30 minutes so the excess can come out.
4. Record the date and amount in the maintenance work order.

## Warning signs
A temperature that rises more than 10 °C after lubrication, dark grease or grease with metal particles, or a noise that appears right after greasing.
