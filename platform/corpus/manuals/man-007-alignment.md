# Motor-pump coupling alignment

## Why it matters
Poor alignment is one of the most common causes of premature bearing and seal failure. It increases vibration at 2× the running frequency and heats up the coupling.

## Tolerances
With laser alignment, the parallel misalignment (offset) must be below 0.05 mm and the angular misalignment below 0.05 mm per 100 mm of coupling diameter. Always measure with the pump at operating temperature.

## Short procedure
1. Apply LOTO and remove the coupling guard.
2. Check for soft foot: no motor foot may lift more than 0.05 mm when the bolts are loosened one by one.
3. Mount the laser heads and measure.
4. Correct the vertical first with calibrated shims, then the horizontal.
5. Tighten the bolts to the specified torque and measure again.
6. Record the final values.

## Piping
The piping must not transmit stress to the casing. If the alignment changes by more than 0.05 mm when the suction flange is loosened, there is pipe strain to correct.
