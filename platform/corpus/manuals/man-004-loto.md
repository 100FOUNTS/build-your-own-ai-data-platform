# Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)

## Purpose
The LOTO (Lockout/Tagout) procedure guarantees that a machine is isolated from every energy source before anyone works on it. It is mandatory for any mechanical or electrical work on a pump.

## The six steps
1. Notify operations and all affected personnel.
2. Switch off the pump with the normal shutdown procedure.
3. Isolate every energy source: the motor breaker and the suction and discharge valves.
4. Put a personal padlock and a tag with name, date and reason on each isolation point. Each technician uses their own padlock; nobody removes another person's padlock.
5. Release stored energy: bleed the casing pressure and wait for the rotor to stop completely.
6. Verify zero energy: try to start from the local panel and confirm the absence of voltage with a tester before touching anything.

## Removing the lockout
Only the technician who put the padlock on may remove it, after checking that the tools have been removed and the area is clear.

## Prohibitions
It is forbidden to work on a pump without LOTO even if the job is short, and it is forbidden to switch off several pumps at once to avoid the procedure.
