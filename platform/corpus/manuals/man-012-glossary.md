# Reliability glossary

**MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures).** Mean operating time between two consecutive failures of the same asset.

**MTTR (Mean Time To Repair).** Mean time a repair takes, from the moment the equipment fails until it is running again.

**NPSH (Net Positive Suction Head).** The available NPSH depends on the installation and the required NPSH depends on the pump; the first must exceed the second.

**RMS.** Root mean square value of a signal; it is the usual way to express overall vibration in mm/s.

**ISO 10816.** Standard that classifies the vibration severity of rotating machines according to their power and type of support.

**LOTO.** Lockout/Tagout: locking and tagging energy sources before working on equipment.

**Criticality.** A, B or C classification of an asset according to the impact of its failure on the process. A is the most critical.

**Predictive maintenance.** A strategy that uses condition data and models to intervene just before the failure, instead of on a fixed calendar or after the breakdown.

**Work order.** Record of a job: preventive, corrective or inspection type, with dates, cost and downtime hours.

**Baseline.** The normal behavior of a variable for an asset, computed over a healthy reference period.
