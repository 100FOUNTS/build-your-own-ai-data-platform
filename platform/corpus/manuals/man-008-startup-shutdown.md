# Startup and shutdown

## Before starting
Check that the suction tank level is above the minimum, that the suction valve is fully open and that the pump is primed (casing full of liquid). Turn the shaft by hand to check that it rotates freely.

## Startup sequence
1. Close the discharge valve or leave it 10 % open.
2. Start the motor and check the direction of rotation the first time.
3. When the discharge pressure stabilizes, slowly open the discharge valve up to the design flow.
4. Check vibration, temperature and leaks during the first 15 minutes.

## Running against a closed discharge
A centrifugal pump must never run with the discharge fully closed for more than 30 seconds: the liquid heats up, the seal gets damaged and cavitation or vaporization can occur.

## Shutdown sequence
Slowly close the discharge valve, stop the motor and let the rotor come to a complete stop before closing the suction. Avoid abrupt stops that cause water hammer in the piping.

## Starts per hour
Limit starts to a maximum of 6 per hour so the motor doesn't overheat.
