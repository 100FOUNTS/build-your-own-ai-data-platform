# Preventive maintenance schedule

## Frequencies
- Visual inspection and parameter round: once a month, 1 hour, without stopping the pump.
- Major preventive maintenance: every 50 to 60 days of operation for criticality B and C pumps, with a 4 to 6 hour shutdown.
- Criticality A pumps: preventive every 45 days, because their failure stops the whole process.

## Contents of the major preventive
Grease change and bearing check, inspection of the mechanical seal and its flush plan, laser alignment check, tightening of the anchor bolts, motor insulation test and cleaning of the suction strainers.

## Planning
Coordinate the shutdown with operations at least 72 hours ahead and confirm that a standby pump covers the service. Do not schedule preventives for two pumps on the same line on the same day.

## Records
Every job is recorded in a work order with type, opening and closing dates, downtime hours and cost. That data feeds the reliability analysis and the training of the predictive models.

## Adjustments
If a pump fails before its interval is up, shorten the next interval by 20 % and analyze the root cause.
