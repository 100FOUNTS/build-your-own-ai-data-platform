Explain **$ARGUMENTS** to someone who is learning engineering and wants to understand the why. Check `guide/glossary.md` in case there is already a definition.

In the learner's language, briefly:
1. **What it is**, in one sentence and without jargon.
2. **An everyday analogy**.
3. **Why it matters**: what breaks or how much it costs if ignored, with an example from this project (pumps, sensors, Kafka, Iceberg, API…).
4. **How it looks on a real job** (tools or practices).
5. **A question** to check they understood. Don't give the answer: wait.
